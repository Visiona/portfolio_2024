UX Case Study

Senior MDs Candidate Form

A secure Power Apps submission flow that replaced a manual PowerPoint process, reduced support workload and made candidate information easier to collect, review and prepare for senior leadership.

UX Design Interaction Design Power Platform
Role UX, interaction designer, Power Platform developer
Team Project manager, project owner, developers and support team
Users Senior MD candidates and leadership support team
Tools Power Apps, SharePoint, InDesign, Teams
{% include project-proof.html project="candidate_form" %}

The challenge

A PowerPoint-based process was creating manual work and communication overload.

The Senior Executive Support team needed to collect input from around 80 Senior Managing Director candidates. The existing workflow used pre-styled PowerPoint templates that required extensive manual editing, resending, copying and pasting before the final C-suite review.

  • Candidate information arrived in inconsistent PowerPoint files
  • The support team spent many hours cleaning and merging content
  • Applicants needed repeated clarification about what to provide
  • The final output had to be polished, accurate and easy for senior leadership to review

Goals & approach

Design a clearer submission process, not just a prettier template.

During the initial brief, I realised that redesigning the PowerPoint would not solve the real problem. The opportunity was to redesign the process itself: how information was requested, entered, stored, validated and prepared for review.

  • Create a new guided submission process
  • Reduce back-and-forth communication with applicants
  • Make the form quicker and easier for candidates to complete
  • Automate and simplify the support team’s preparation work
  • Improve readability for leadership reviewers

Research

The core problem was not the slide design. It was the broken workflow around it.

01

Manual preparation

Support teams had to edit, merge and polish many submitted slides before review.

02

Inconsistent input

Candidates interpreted the PowerPoint fields differently, causing rework and clarification.

03

High communication load

Applicants and support teams exchanged repeated messages to resolve missing or unclear details.

04

Executive review pressure

The final candidate packs needed to be accurate, polished and easy to scan by senior leaders.

Discovery process

Observe, map, ideate, validate.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Reviewed the existing PowerPoint process and observed how the support team prepared candidate packs.

  2. 02

    Interview

    Interviewed support team members to understand pain points, bright spots and workflow constraints.

  3. 03

    Ideate

    Ran a design thinking session with designers, developers and the project manager to explore solutions.

  4. 04

    Test

    Validated an MVP with stakeholders and candidates, then iterated the form and supporting flow.

Research artefacts

Mapping the existing process before redesigning the future one.

MVP solution

Build directly in Power Apps to test the real constraints early.

I chose Power Apps because it was already available to Accenture employees and could connect directly to SharePoint lists for storing form data. Building the prototype in the target technology helped avoid surprises caused by Power Apps styling and interaction limitations.

Proposed flow & testing

A new process for applicants, support teams and reviewers.

Interaction design

Small interface decisions reduced errors and gave users more control.

Live application

The final flow connected candidate input to the support team’s preparation work.

The final solution turned a static, manual PowerPoint process into a structured digital form and workflow. Candidate data could be collected more consistently, stored securely and prepared more efficiently for review.

Reflection

The useful solution was a better system, not just a better screen.

The biggest shift in this project was moving from a visual redesign request to a process redesign. By looking at the full workflow, we could remove repetitive manual work, improve clarity for candidates and create a more reliable path to senior review.
