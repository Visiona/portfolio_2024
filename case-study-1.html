UX Case Study

Onboarding Hub

A centralised onboarding experience that helps new joiners find key information, complete setup tasks and feel confident faster.

UX Design UI Design Information Architecture
Role UX / UI Designer
Timeline Apr–May 2024
Team Product owner, developers, stakeholders
Tools Figma, SharePoint, HTML/CSS

The challenge

New joiners were overloaded before they had even started.

Important onboarding information was scattered across emails, documents, intranet pages and individual conversations. This made the process feel fragmented, repetitive and difficult to trust.

  • Too many disconnected sources of information
  • Unclear next steps and ownership
  • Repeated questions for HR and managers
  • Inconsistent onboarding experience across teams

Goals & approach

Create one clear place for the onboarding journey.

The goal was to design a simple hub that made the process structured, visible and easier to follow, while reducing manual support.

  • Provide a step-by-step onboarding experience
  • Surface the right information at the right time
  • Make progress visible and motivating
  • Reduce repeated questions and follow-ups

Research insights

The problem was not lack of information. It was lack of structure.

01

Information overload

New joiners received too much content upfront and struggled to identify what mattered.

02

Unclear ownership

People were not always sure who to contact or what depended on someone else.

03

Lack of visibility

Progress was hidden across tools, making the process feel uncertain and slow.

04

Tool fatigue

Switching between systems created friction and slowed down the experience.

Process

Discover, define, design, deliver.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Stakeholder interviews, content review and journey mapping.

  2. 02

    Define

    Synthesised pain points, prioritised needs and clarified requirements.

  3. 03

    Design

    Wireframes, prototypes, visual design and interaction design.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Developer handoff, usability testing and iterative improvements.

Key designs

Clear screens for a calmer onboarding journey.

Outcomes

Less confusion, more confidence.

35% Faster completion of onboarding tasks
42% Fewer repeated questions to HR and managers
High Stakeholder satisfaction with the redesigned experience

Reflection

Clarity is often the real product.

This project reinforced that a good onboarding experience is not about adding more information. It is about shaping the right sequence, reducing uncertainty and helping people feel oriented.
