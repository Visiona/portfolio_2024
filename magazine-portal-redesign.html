UX Case Study

Magazine Portal Redesign

A clearer content model and reading experience for a support-focused publication serving people at different stages of the 12-step journey.

UX Research Information Architecture UI Design Prototyping
Role UX Research and UI Design
Client AA Fellowship
Timeline 2024 · Ongoing
Focus Content structure and readability
{% include project-proof.html project="magazine" %}

The challenge

A valuable publication was difficult to explore.

The existing magazine website lacked a dependable content hierarchy. Readers had to work too hard to understand what was available, locate relevant support material and move between current and archived content.

Approach

Let user needs define the structure.

Surveys, interviews and basic analytics helped identify reading patterns and content expectations. Those findings informed a new site architecture, wireframes and a prototype designed around clear routes into the publication.

Design direction

A calmer portal built around reading and discovery.

The proposed design gives editorial content more room, reduces visual competition and creates predictable pathways between topics, issues and support resources.

Current outcome

A stronger foundation before implementation.

The redesigned architecture and prototype have received positive stakeholder feedback. The project remains in progress, with the validated structure providing a practical foundation for content migration and development.
